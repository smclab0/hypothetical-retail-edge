SUSE Edge retail lab · vrack0

retail-shed estate

An HQ Rancher cluster with no load-balancer VMs and seven stores: six on isolated store networks and Liverpool on the HQ LAN, where a phone can open its till. One till app reaches all of them from Git, and every store connection to HQ is outbound.

How a box joins a store

One image, retail-store.iso, is used for every SL Micro box. The Glasgow kiosk installs SLES 16 with GNOME through an unattended Agama profile that carries the same enrolment service. Every box gets its identity from the SMBIOS serial at first boot.

  1. HQ creates the store record. setup-stores.sh makes an empty K3s cluster store-man-001 labelled with store, region and tier.
  2. Combustion installs the enrolment service on first boot: HQ CA anchor, read-only token in /etc, program in /usr/libexec.
  3. The box names itself from its serial, store-man-001-n1, and trusts the HQ CA.
  4. It waits for its record and reads the registration command with a token that can only list clusters and registration tokens. It retries through WAN outages.
  5. It runs the verified command (CA checksum, no --insecure) with all roles. Rancher installs K3s v1.35.8 and the agents.
  6. Fleet sees the new cluster's labels and deploys store-pos with that store's values.
